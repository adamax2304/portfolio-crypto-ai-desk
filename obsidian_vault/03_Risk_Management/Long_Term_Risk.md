# Long-Term Risk

## Status

The desk has no long-term mode (see [[Long_Term_Mode]]). The risk concepts below are recommendations for when you operate longer-horizon positions outside the desk's tactical scope.

## Why long-term is different

- Position-sized for weeks / months. A 50% drawdown is acceptable if the thesis holds.
- Invalidation is structural, not ATR-based: usually weekly close past a level or breaking a multi-month low.
- News risk dominates over technical risk — a single negative catalyst can change the thesis.
- Funding / OI is almost irrelevant at long horizon.

## Recommended discipline

1. **Thesis sentence** — write it. If you can't, you don't have a long-term position.
2. **Anchor exchange only** — never custody long-term on MEXC or unfamiliar exchanges.
3. **Cold storage above some threshold** — anything you don't need on exchange should be off exchange.
4. **Macro overlay** — track ETF flows, dollar, real rates. The desk only does keyword macro; you need a real macro feed.
5. **Narrative cool-down rule** — re-evaluate every 4 weeks; if narrative cooled and price action is range-bound for > 8 weeks, exit.
6. **Stablecoin floor** — keep a dry-powder allocation. The desk doesn't track this.

## Long-term risks the desk does NOT model

- Token unlocks beyond the 7-day window.
- Regulatory action timing.
- Exchange solvency.
- Custodial counterparty risk.
- Quarterly / annual macro events.

## Use the desk for entries, not for the thesis

For a long-term position you already believe in, use the desk to time entries / adds: wait for an intraday-friendly pullback to scale in, not a vertical melt-up.
