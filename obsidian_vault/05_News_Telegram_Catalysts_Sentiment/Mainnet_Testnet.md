# Mainnet / Testnet

## Status

`CatalystAgent` recognizes `type: mainnet` and `type: testnet` as catalysts. The bridge tags `mainnet`, `upgrade` as bullish.

## Why these matter

- A long-anticipated mainnet launch is the canonical "buy the rumor, sell the news" pattern in crypto.
- Testnets matter less for price but matter for project credibility.
- Mainnet upgrades (forks, version bumps) can change tokenomics or unlock new features that affect demand.

## Evaluation

1. **Confirmed date** — verified via project blog or governance? Or just rumor?
2. **Roadmap on schedule** — has the project hit prior milestones, or are they chronically late?
3. **Pre-mainnet narrative cycle** — has there been a multi-week run-up?
4. **Token utility change** — does mainnet unlock staking, governance, fees?

## Trade patterns

- **Run-up into mainnet** — often 30–50% over the prior 2–4 weeks. Late entries are crowded.
- **Mainnet day flush** — frequent. Avoid taking longs in the 24h before mainnet unless structure is exceptional.
- **Post-mainnet drift** — variable. Tradable both directions depending on broader regime.

## Operator rules

- Within 48h of a confirmed mainnet, downgrade swing longs to watchlist.
- After a mainnet flush, wait for the second-leg setup; do not catch the first falling knife.
- Testnet is rarely tradable; mostly informational.

## Logging

- Track the mainnet date as a manual event with `type: mainnet`.
- Pre-event: file Telegram chatter in [[Telegram_News]].
- Post-event: classify outcome in [[Market_Moving_News]] or [[Useless_News]].
