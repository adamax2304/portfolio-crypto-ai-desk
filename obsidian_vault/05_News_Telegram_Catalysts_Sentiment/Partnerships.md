# Partnerships

## Status in the desk

`CatalystAgent` recognizes events of `type: partnership`. It's tagged as a regular catalyst (not a risk event). `news_trader_bridge` flags `partnership` and `integration` as bullish keywords.

## Why partnerships are tricky

- Often vague — a "strategic partnership" with no specifics rarely moves price meaningfully.
- Often overhyped pre-announcement.
- Sometimes priced in days before the announcement.
- Frequently produce a quick spike + fade.

## How to evaluate one

1. **Counterparty quality** — is the partner a Fortune 500 / major financial institution / large protocol, or a tiny no-name?
2. **Concrete deliverable** — actual integration with timeline, or marketing fluff?
3. **Token utility increase** — does the token get a real use case, or is the partnership token-irrelevant?
4. **Already announced before** — many "new" partnerships are re-announcements.
5. **Source primary** — is it on the project's own blog or just a press release?

## Trading rules

- Partnerships rarely justify a fresh A-tier entry on their own; they bump attention.
- Skip the announcement candle; wait for the retest / acceptance.
- Watch for sell-the-news flush 5–15 minutes after the headline.

## Logging

- File partnership Telegram messages in [[Telegram_News/README|Telegram_News]] first.
- After 4–24h, move to [[Useful_News]] or [[Useless_News]] based on whether the move had legs.
