# Bitcoin Ecosystem

## Status

**Not currently configured.** Recommended addition.

## What this sector includes (typical)

- STX (Stacks), ORDI, SATS, RUNE (sometimes claimed but not exclusive).
- BRC-20 / Runes tokens.

## What usually moves the sector

- BTC price action — when BTC pumps, the ecosystem follows hard.
- Bitcoin L2 narrative cycles.
- BTC ETF flows.
- Halving narrative windows.

## What news matters

- BTC-native L2 mainnets / upgrades.
- New Runes / BRC-20 high-volume launches.
- ETF / institutional BTC headlines (lifts ecosystem).

## Telegram / social signals

- Active for Ordinals / Runes; quieter for STX.

## Best regime

- `bullish trend` with BTC leading.
- Worst: `bearish trend` — these are very high beta.

## Common risk

- Many tokens are not on Binance — `not_on_anchor` or MEXC-only caps.
- Liquidity thin on Runes / BRC-20.

## Common fake signals

- "Bitcoin L2 launching soon" hype with no working product.
- BRC-20 pumps that have no underlying.
