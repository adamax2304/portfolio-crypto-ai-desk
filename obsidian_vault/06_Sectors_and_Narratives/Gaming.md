# Gaming (GameFi)

Configured narrative (keywords only). Keywords: gaming, gamefi.

## What this sector includes (typical)

- IMX, AXS, BEAM, GALA, RON, PIXEL, MAVIA.

## What usually moves the sector

- Major game launches.
- Console / mobile distribution wins.
- BTC risk-on regime (high-beta narrative).
- Specific airdrops to gamers.

## What news matters

- Real player counts / DAUs.
- Major studio partnerships.
- Network performance for game-native chains.

## Telegram / social signals

- Active Telegram around specific games; quality varies.
- Watch for player-base sentiment vs token-holder sentiment — they can diverge.

## Best regime

- `bullish trend`.
- Gaming has its own seasonal cycle (Q4 / holidays sometimes lift sentiment).

## Common risk

- Tokenomics often inflationary (rewards models).
- Game updates / nerfs can crater token utility.

## Common fake signals

- "Next big AAA game" announcements with no demo.
- Influencer-driven pumps on small-cap gaming tokens.

## Scoring posture

- Keywords-only configuration; weak symbol mapping → narrative contribution weaker.
