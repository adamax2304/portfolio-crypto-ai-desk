# How Sectors Behave By Regime

A compact reference. Each sector × regime combo with the most useful posture.

| Sector | Bullish trend | Bearish trend | Range/chop | Transition | High-vol danger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI | longs preferred; chase leaders | avoid longs; selective shorts | range fades | wait | no trade |
| RWA | slower long entries OK | hold cash | range OK | wait | reduce |
| L2 | track ETH leadership | avoid longs | range OK | wait | no trade |
| Solana ecosystem | high-beta longs | flush risk; avoid longs | scalp only | wait | no trade |
| Base ecosystem | longs OK on anchor-listed | avoid; thin liquidity | small only | wait | no trade |
| Memes | rotation longs in `early_attention` | aggressive avoid; potential shorts | scalp fades | wait | no trade |
| DePIN | swing longs on RNDR-style names | avoid | range OK | wait | no trade |
| Gaming | longs OK on majors | underperform; avoid | range OK | wait | no trade |
| Perp DEX | longs OK on Binance-listed | avoid; thin | scalp OK | wait | no trade |
| Bitcoin ecosystem | high-beta longs | flush risk | small only | wait | no trade |
| Exchange tokens | longs OK | regulatory headline risk | range OK | wait | no trade |
| Stablecoin related | yield-driven longs | hold | mean revert | wait | no trade |
| Privacy | macro fear can boost | typically weak | range | wait | no trade |
| New listings | scalp only; cap risk | avoid | scalp only | wait | no trade |

## Rules of thumb

- High-beta sectors (AI, Memes, Solana, Bitcoin ecosystem) demand `bullish trend` to be safe.
- Low-beta sectors (RWA, Stablecoins, Privacy) can sometimes work in chop / transition.
- All sectors → no trade in `high-volatility danger`.
- Watchlist staging during `transition` is the highest-EV behavior.
