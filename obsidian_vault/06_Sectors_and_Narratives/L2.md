# L2 (Layer 2)

Configured narrative. Symbols: ARB, OP. Keywords: layer 2, rollup, optimistic, arbitrum, optimism.

## What usually moves this sector

- ETH performance — L2 tokens are correlated with ETH.
- Major airdrops, new rollup launches.
- TVL flows into specific L2s (DeFiLlama).
- Sequencer decentralization or fee changes.

## What news matters

- L2 protocol upgrades.
- Major dApp launches on a specific L2.
- Coinbase Base / Linea / Scroll activity (ARB / OP can lag or lead the narrative).

## Telegram / social signals

- Twitter / X drives most L2 conversation; Telegram channels are quieter.
- Watch for "ARB unlock" or "OP unlock" — see [[Token_Unlocks]].

## Best regime

- Same as ETH — `bullish trend` with breadth.
- Worst: `bearish trend` — L2s usually underperform ETH on the way down.

## Indicators that matter most

- Relative strength vs ETH (not just BTC).
- Daily TVL trend (not in desk; check externally).

## Common risk

- Unlocks — both ARB and OP have ongoing unlock schedules.
- L2 narrative cycles: rotation between L2s, not all rise together.

## Common fake signals

- "Modular blockchain" buzzword spam.
- Hype around new rollups that have no traction.

## Scoring posture

- Narrative weight 10 base; swing × 1.30.

## Example good setup

- ARB in `bullish trend`, RS vs ETH > 0, no unlock within 7 days, breakout retest of EMA20 with volume confirmation.

## Example bad setup

- OP within 3 days of a major unlock; ScoringAgent risk_events penalty active.
