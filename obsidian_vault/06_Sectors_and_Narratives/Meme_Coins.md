# Meme Coins

Configured narrative. Symbols: DOGE, PEPE, WIF. Keywords: meme, dog, cat, pepe, wif.

## What usually moves this sector

- Attention. Memes are pure attention-driven.
- Twitter / Telegram hype waves.
- BTC risk-on regime.
- Specific catalysts: listings, airdrops on meme-heavy ecosystems.

## What news matters

- Major-exchange listings for memes (Coinbase listed PEPE = catalyst).
- Cultural moments (movies, celebrities, viral content).
- Meme aggregator funds / ETFs (rare).

## What does NOT matter

- Project roadmaps. Memes don't have them.
- Tokenomics changes. Mostly irrelevant.
- TVL or "real-world adoption" headlines.

## Telegram / social signals

- **Highest** signal-to-noise. Memes live on Telegram.
- Crowd attention stage (`growing_hype`, `euphoria`) is the most predictive variable here.
- `news_trader_bridge` Telegram mentions correlate well with short-term moves on memes.

## Best regime

- `bullish trend` with breadth ≥ 60.
- `range/chop` can produce fast scalp moves but is dangerous.
- Worst: `bearish trend` — memes lead the down move; do not buy.

## Indicators that matter most

- Volume_ratio (must be 1.5+ to consider).
- `CrowdAttentionAgent` stage.
- Funding rate (extreme positive = exit signal).

## Common risk

- Vertical melt-ups followed by 30%+ flushes.
- MEXC-only listings cap at B-tier; many memes only trade on MEXC.
- Extreme funding (>0.001) crowding into the trade.
- Crowd stage `euphoria` warns explicitly.

## Common fake signals

- Pump groups coordinating Telegram blasts.
- Tweets from "influencers" who are exiting positions.
- Repeated old "moon soon" hype.

## Scoring posture

- Narrative weight 10 base; meme-heavy ideas often hit MEXC-only or extreme funding caps before reaching A.

## Example good setup

- WIF in `bullish trend`, RS vs SOL > 0, volume_ratio 2x, funding ~0, crowd stage `early_attention` or `growing_hype`.

## Example bad setup

- A new MEXC-only meme with `tier=single_source` Telegram hype, funding +0.001, crowd stage `euphoria` — capped by MEXC + extreme funding.
