# Privacy Coins

## Status

**Not currently configured.** Recommended addition.

## What this covers

- XMR (Monero), ZEC (Zcash), DASH, also Railgun / Aztec where listed.

## What usually moves the sector

- Regulatory pressure (often bearish — exchange delistings).
- Privacy-narrative moments (financial surveillance debates, CBDC backlash).
- Geopolitical events (capital controls, sanctions, war).

## What news matters

- Major exchange delistings of privacy coins (often bearish but sometimes bullish as scarcity).
- Government policy on privacy / surveillance.
- New ZK / privacy tech launches.

## Telegram / social signals

- Smaller / more dedicated communities; less hype-prone.

## Best regime

- Counter-cyclical — sometimes moves on macro fear (capital flight narratives).
- `high-volatility danger` + macro risk-off can lift privacy coins briefly.

## Common risk

- Delisting risk (Binance has delisted some in the past).
- Liquidity thinning over time.

## Common fake signals

- "Privacy is back" headlines that don't sustain.
