# RWA (Real-World Assets)

Configured narrative. Symbols: ONDO, LINK. Keywords: real world assets, tokenization, treasuries.

## What usually moves this sector

- US Treasury yield headlines.
- Major financial institution adoption (BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, JPM).
- Regulatory clarity on tokenized securities.
- Stablecoin / on-chain credit volume.

## What news matters

- BlackRock / large asset manager tokenization announcements.
- Treasury auction news + yields.
- SEC rulings on tokenized securities.

## Telegram / social signals

- Lower hype frequency than memes / AI — when it does trend, more likely to be real.
- LINK has strong Telegram presence; ONDO is more institutional.

## Best regime

- Mixed — RWA doesn't need a screaming bull tape; it benefits from "search for yield" macro.
- Worst: `high-volatility danger` (RWA correlates with risk-on but slower).

## Indicators that matter most

- LINK relative strength as proxy for RWA strength.
- Slower volume — multi-day average, not single bar.

## Common risk

- News-driven gaps; gaps fade quickly without follow-through.
- Lower beta than memes / AI — sizing must scale up to find R/R.

## Common fake signals

- Generic "tokenization is the future" headlines.
- Old institutional partnerships re-announced.

## Scoring posture

- Narrative weight 10 base; in swing × 1.30.

## Example good setup

- LINK in `bullish trend` after a verified BlackRock tokenization headline, retest of EMA20 with rising volume.

## Example bad setup

- ONDO chasing a single Twitter post about "tokenized treasuries" with no primary source.
