# Solana Ecosystem

## Status

**Not currently configured** in `config/watchlist.yaml.narratives`. Recommended addition (see [[Sector_Overview]]).

## What usually moves this sector

- SOL price action. The ecosystem is essentially a SOL beta play.
- Major airdrops / launches on Solana (JUP, JTO, W, Pyth, etc.).
- Network performance / outage news.
- Meme coin rotation on Solana (BONK, WIF, POPCAT).

## What news matters

- Solana mainnet stability headlines.
- Big launches (Jupiter, Helium-on-Solana, etc.).
- ETF-track headlines for SOL.

## Telegram / social signals

- Very active Telegram presence. Memes side of the ecosystem is loud — high false-positive rate.
- Whale wallet flows from `lookonchain` style channels are useful.

## Best regime

- `bullish trend` with risk-on bias. Solana is a high-beta cluster.
- Worst: `bearish trend` — the entire cluster moves together down.

## Indicators that matter most

- SOL relative strength vs BTC.
- Solana memes (BONK, WIF) leading or lagging SOL.

## Common risk

- High correlation inside the cluster — treat as single bet for risk-sizing.
- Network outages (rare but real); historic precedent.

## Common fake signals

- Random Solana meme pumps on illiquid CEXs.
- "Insider" listing rumors for Solana tokens.

## Scoring posture

- Would behave like AI / Memes once configured.

## Example good setup

- SOL leading, JUP and JTO with positive RS, regime bullish.

## Example bad setup

- A small Solana meme that only trades on MEXC, with rumor-only catalyst.
