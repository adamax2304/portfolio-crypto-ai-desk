# Fake_or_Unverified_News

Items that turned out to be false, or remained unverified after the relevant window.

## Criteria

- No primary-source confirmation within 24h.
- OR contradicted by a primary source.
- OR explicitly retracted.

## Why log them

- These are the highest-EV pattern to recognize. Channels that produce repeated fake or unverified items should be muted.
- Captures rumor archetypes that recur (e.g. fake listing leaks).

## What to capture

Use [[News_Item_Template]] and explicitly mark:

- `Verification status: Fake` or `Unverified`.
- `Did the market care?` — sometimes the market reacts to fakes anyway, briefly.
- `What this teaches about source X`.
