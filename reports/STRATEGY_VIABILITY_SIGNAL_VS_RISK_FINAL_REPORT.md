## Bottom line
The system is **not viable as currently run** (its profitability is a touch-based illusion; real exits lose −96…−137R). But the signals are **not noise** — they reach +0.5R favorable more than half the time. The loss comes from **risk management: a fixed stop with no protection in the zone where most of the favorable move lives.** Add **early MFE-armed protection (BE at +0.6R, lock +0.5R at +1R)** and the actionable book turns **+74R / PF 1.43** in replay — broadly, across cohorts, not from one symbol. This is the most hopeful, and most actionable, finding of the three audits: **fix the exits (early protection) + shadow the worst chase-long cohort, validate in paper across a fresh epoch with costs, then decide.** Caveat hard: 30 days, one epoch, hindsight trigger, costs unmodeled — promising, not proven.
