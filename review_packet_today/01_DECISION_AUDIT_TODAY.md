**Useful for real decision-making?**
- As a **screening + structuring tool**, yes. The pipeline lists levels, rejects bad ideas with explicit reasons, and respects NO TRADE.
- As an **autonomous decision system**, no. Counter-thesis is shallow, news is uneven, signal feedback loop is silent, and packets are stale.

- **M7.** Scalp ideas lack explicit 5m execution triggers (only pullback zones).
- **M8.** Intraday crypto ideas lack 4h structural read.
